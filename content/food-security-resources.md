In the coming posts, we will examine what resources are actually needed to secure food for the future. 

How much food will we actually need to produce for what number of people? What demands will be put upon water and land? How much food is lost currently, both during harvests and during distribution? How do people and organisations consume food (and should they change)? 

As those are many questions, this section on _Resources_ will be shorter and, hopefully, more succinct than the general food security debate.
