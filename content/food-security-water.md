We've talked about demand for food once the population rises, especially in sub-Saharan Africa. We've also discussed challenges such as food loss.\
Let's now turn to the question of specific resources; first up is water.

Water is present in many forms. As oceans, groundwater, ice, fresh water from rivers and lakes, permafrost, moisture in the soil and water in the atmosphere. The relationships between these entities (evaporation in the ocean, evapotranspiration from the soil, runoff from groundwater to the sea, and precipitation above land and oceans) form the water cycle.
