We've established that water is critically important to food security, but plants aren't (usually) grown in a vacuum - they require space and healthy soil. This section examines if these resources are sufficient.

Firstly, agricultural land is defined as cropland and pasture for grazing animals. The rest of the land is covered by forests or land used for other purposes (such as human habitation). Land covered in ice is excluded.
